Standard Operating Procedure (SOP)

1
Field & Soil Preparation

Perform deep summer ploughing (25-30 cm) followed by two cross-harrowings to break clods. Incorporate 10-15 tonnes of well-rotted Farm Yard Manure (FYM) per hectare before the final harrowing to enrich organic carbon.

2
Seed Treatment & Sowing Window

Treat certified seeds with Trichoderma viride @ 4g/kg seed or Carbendazim @ 2g/kg seed to prevent seed-borne fungal infections. Maintain optimal row-to-row spacing of 20-25cm and plant-to-plant spacing of 10-15cm.

3
Nutrient & Irrigation Schedule

Apply N:P:K as per soil test recommendations. Deliver 50% nitrogen along with 100% phosphorus and potash as basal dose. Apply remaining nitrogen in two equal splits at active vegetative and panicle/flowering initiation.

4
Integrated Pest & Disease Management

Regularly inspect for stem borer, leaf blast, and sucking pests. Install pheromone traps @ 5 per acre. In case of severe infestation, spray recommended bio-pesticides (Neem oil 1500 ppm @ 3ml/L) or targeted university-approved actives.

5
Harvesting & Post-Harvest Storage

Harvest when 80-85% of ears/pods turn golden straw-colored and grain moisture drops to ~18-20%. Sun-dry harvested grains to 12% moisture prior to storage in hermetic bags to prevent weevil damage.
